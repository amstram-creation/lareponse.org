## Structure

1. **Homepage**
   - Brief overview of the company
   - Key services offered
   - Call to action (CTA)

2. **About Us**
   - Company history
   - Mission and vision
   - Ethical considerations

3. **Services**
   - List of services offered
   - Description of each service
   - How it addresses client needs

4. **Portfolio**
   - Past projects
   - Testimonials from clients

5. **Blog**
   - Articles related to web development, ethical tech, etc.
  
6. **Contact**
   - Contact information
   - Inquiry form

7. **Footer**
   - Quick links
   - Social media icons
   - Legal information (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service)
